**Patterns and Recurring Elements:**

*   **Drizzle ORM Adoption:** The project heavily leverages Drizzle ORM for database schema definition, migrations, and relationships.
*   **Modular Database Design:** Schemas are organized into individual files within `src/db/schema/`, and relationships are defined in separate `*-relations.ts` files, all exported through a central `src/db/schema/index.ts` barrel file.
*   **UUID Primary Keys:** All primary keys are `uuid`s with `defaultRandom()` generation.
*   **Timestamps and Soft Deletion:** Most tables consistently include `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, and `deletedAt` for robust data management and auditing, facilitated by a `helpers.ts` file.
*   **Indexing for Performance:** Frequent addition of indexes on foreign keys, unique fields (like `slug`, `email`, `sku`), and frequently queried columns (`status`, `createdAt`) is a recurring practice.
*   **Enum Usage:** `pgEnum` is widely used for status fields (user, product, variant), types (address, note, transaction), and specific characteristics (fragrance concentration), enhancing data integrity and clarity.
*   **API-First / Domain-Driven Architecture:** The `setup-project.sh` script reveals an intention for a modular, layered architecture (domain, application, infrastructure, presentation) for various business modules, hinting at a robust, scalable backend design.
*   **Iterative Schema Refinement:** The `products.schema.ts` file shows a notable pattern of iterative changes and corrections, particularly around the `isVisible` property, suggesting an evolving design process for certain features.
*   **Comprehensive Features:** The wide range of schemas (users, roles, permissions, sessions, customers, addresses, audit, brands, categories, products, variants, inventory, pricing, images, fragrance notes, inventory transactions) indicates the development of a full-fledged e-commerce platform with robust administrative and catalog features.
